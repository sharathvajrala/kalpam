Kalpam

Mee Sankalpam — prathi roju, prathi vratam.

Kalpam brings your god's sannidhanam into your home: daily pooja, vratams and chanting, guided step by step — in Telugu and English, made for elders and families alike.

3D Sannidhanam

Do pooja with your own hands in a 3D sanctum — light the deepam, shower flowers, pour abhishekam, break the coconut, wave the aarti. Or choose Watch Mode and let the full pooja be performed for you.

Daily Rituals

Morning and evening rituals anchored to your city's sunrise and sunset — Sankalpam, Suprabhatam, deepam, archana and aarti. Choose your god, or let the day choose: Monday Shiva, Tuesday Hanuman, Wednesday Ayyappa, Thursday Sai Baba, Friday Lakshmi & Durga, Saturday Venkateswara, Sunday Surya.

Vratams & Malas

Every season has its flagship vratam — Ayyappa Mandala Deeksha, Satyanarayana, Ekadashi, Varalakshmi and more — with katha, samagri lists, fasting guidance and a gentle day-by-day tracker.

Grantham & Bhajans

The stotra canon in-app — Vishnu Sahasranamam, Lalitha Sahasranamam, Hanuman Chalisa, Ashtottarams — with chant-along lines, meanings, a japa mala counter, and bhajans with lyrics for your god.
