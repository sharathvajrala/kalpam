Privacy Policy — Kalpam

Last updated: 27 November 2026

Kalpam ("the app") is a devotional app for daily pooja, vratams, chanting and bhajans. Kalpam is designed to be private by architecture: the core app works fully on your device, with no account and no advertising.

What the app stores on your device

The following information is stored only on your device and is used only to personalise your pooja:

  • Your chosen language and chosen god (or "by day" mode)
  • Your city name — used to compute sunrise, sunset and Panchangam for your location
  • Your name, gotram and, if you choose to add it, your janma nakshatram — spoken in your Sankalpam. You may create several family profiles on one device.
  • Your morning and evening reminder times
  • Your practice data: vratam and mala progress, ritual completion, chanting counts

This data never leaves your device unless you choose to enable the optional account features described below.

Location

During setup you may allow a one-time, approximate location check so the app can suggest your nearest city. Your coordinates are not stored and your location history is never kept. Only the city name you confirm is saved, on your device.

Microphone and voice commands

If you use voice commands (for example in Elder Mode), the app uses your device's built-in speech recognition only while you hold the microphone button. Kalpam does not record, store or transmit your audio or any transcript — recognition is performed by your device's own speech service.

Notifications

Reminders (morning and evening rituals, vratam check-ins, festival notices) are scheduled locally on your device from its own clock and your chosen city. They do not require a server.

Optional account features

No account is needed for pooja, chanting, bhajans or trackers. If, in a future version, you choose to join a group or turn on backup/sync, you may be asked for a phone number or email address for sign-in. That information would be used only to operate those features, and this policy will be updated before those features launch.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, and no ad tracking
  • No sale or sharing of personal data with third parties for marketing
  • No collection of contacts, photos, payment details or government ID
  • No location tracking or location history

Your control

In the app, Settings → Your data lets you export everything the app stores about you, or delete it all with one tap. Deleting removes every stored item on the device and returns the app to first-time setup.

Children

Kalpam is intended for users aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
